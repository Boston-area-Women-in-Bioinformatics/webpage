Every dataset, pipeline, and model reflects the assumptions of its creators. **What scientific knowledge might we have today if women's perspectives had been fully represented throughout history?** Join Boston Women in Bioinformatics for an evening of dinner, a panel discussion, networking, and a raffle and silent auction with researchers, industry scientists, and data professionals exploring this question through the lens of federal policy, data infrastructure, reproductive and consumer health research, the $2.6B women's health VC landscape, and the future of the field.

This is one of our rare ticketed events. Proceeds support BWIB's year-round programming, networking events, and community resources.

## Schedule

- 5:00–5:45 PM: Registration & Networking
- 5:45–6:30 PM: Opening Remarks (Yevgenia Khodor, BWIB President; Gold Sponsor Spotlights; Eli Lilly)
- 6:30–7:30 PM: Panel & Q&A
- 7:30–9:00 PM: Dinner, Networking, Raffle & Silent Auction
