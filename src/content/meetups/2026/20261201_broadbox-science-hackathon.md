Can AI agents make scientific discoveries? Join us at the Broad Institute for a two-day hackathon bringing together experimental scientists, computational researchers, and AI developers to explore that question!

Bring an open question from your research and build a science sandbox where AI agents can run experiments, learn from results, and test hypotheses.

- **Day 1 (December 1):** build your sandbox using the hackathon's reusable infrastructure.
- **Day 2 (December 2):** develop and evaluate AI agents within it.

**No prior AI experience required!** Join individually or with a team of 2–4.

The hackathon will feature an opening keynote from **Eric Lander**, hands-on workshops, mentorship, breakfast, lunch, and prizes. Participants will also receive **Claude credits through Anthropic's AI for Science program** and **Devin credits from Cognition**, with sponsorship from **Merck**, **Benchling**, **Beakr**, and **Lyra**.
