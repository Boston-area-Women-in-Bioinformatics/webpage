The Women's Health Research Hub invites you to the **2026 Women's Health Research Hub Conference**, a full-day symposium bringing together leading scientists, clinicians, engineers, and public health innovators from MIT, Harvard, Mass General Brigham, and beyond. This conference is dedicated to showcasing cutting-edge research and accelerating solutions in sex-specific medicine, engineering, AI, biology across the lifespan, and women's health policy.

### Requirements

- **Registration:** Open to all researchers, students, faculty, industry professionals, and members of the public interested in advancing women’s health research. Advance registration is required as space is limited.
- **Call for Abstracts:** Trainees (undergraduate/graduate students, postdocs, and fellows) wishing to present a poster or flash talk must submit their abstracts by **Monday, October 26th**.

### What You Will Get Out of It

- **Keynote Insights:** Attend an exclusive fireside chat with Kathryn Rexrode (Chief, Division of Women's Health at Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard Medical School), moderated by Frida Polli.
- **Expert Panels & Talks:** Gain cross-disciplinary knowledge across key tracks:
  - **Engineering & AI in Women's Health** featuring Don Ingber, Sangeeta Bhatia, and Bharti Khurana
  - **Biology Across the Lifespan** featuring David Pepin, Kara McKinley, and Jill Goldstein
  - **Public Health & Diagnostics** featuring Smita Gopinath and Shruthi Mahalingaiah
- **Publishing & Funding Guidance:** Hear directly from top journal editors (including _NEJM_ and _Cell_) and industry investors on navigating publication and securing research funding.
- **Trainee Showcases:** Discover pioneering work during the Trainee Lightning Talks and poster session.
- **Networking:** Connect with academic leaders, industry founders, and fellow researchers during dedicated networking sessions, breakfast, lunch, and the evening appetizer reception.

### Getting There

- **Public Transit (MBTA):** Take the **MBTA Red Line** to **Kendall/MIT Station**. The Schwarzman College of Computing is a short 3- to 5-minute walk from the station along Main Street and Vassar Street.
- **Driving & Parking:** Paid public parking is available at the **Kendall Center Garage** (350 Kendall St) or the **55 Franklin St Garage**. On-street metered parking around Vassar St and Main St is extremely limited, so public transportation is strongly encouraged.
