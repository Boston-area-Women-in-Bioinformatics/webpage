**Boston Debate Showcase: MIT faculty debate AI at Laugh Boston**

SuperDebate and Laugh Boston present an evening of live debate about artificial intelligence. MIT Sloan's Yasheng Huang and Fiona Murray (Chair, NATO Innovation Fund) debate US–China AI competition, and Manolis Kellis (MIT CSAIL and the Broad Institute), who uses AI to understand and treat human disease, is also on the program.

**What you get**

- A live debate between leading MIT faculty
- Audience Q&A, and you help judge the debate
- A reception afterwards to meet other people interested in AI

**Details**

- Tuesday 17 November 2026. Doors 5:30pm, program 6pm, ends about 9:30pm
- Laugh Boston, 425 Summer St, Boston (inside the Westin Boston Seaport, next to the convention center; about a 10-minute walk from South Station)
- Early bird $30, general $40; groups of 5+ get 25% off
- 16+ venue; ID checked at the door
