Last fall, [Liyang Diao, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/liyang-diao-90933461/) and [Michael Seiler, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelwseiler/) jumped feet-first into building [Sprout Informatics](https://www.sproutinformatics.com/), a fractional computational biology and bioinformatics consultancy for early-stage biotechs.

You might be wondering: after the initial honeymoon period, how did it go?

## What to Expect

Join us for a candid conversation about what the first year actually looked like. Topics could include:

- Finding their niche
- Pricing, scoping, and other things they did wrong
- Uncertainty: what you can live with, and what you can't
- What they miss (and what they don't) from working the 9-to-5

We'll kick off with a few anchor questions, then open the floor to you.

## Who Should Attend

Whether you're thinking about consulting, starting something of your own, or just curious what life outside a single company looks like, bring your questions. Nothing is off-limits (except, as noted, their five-year plan).
