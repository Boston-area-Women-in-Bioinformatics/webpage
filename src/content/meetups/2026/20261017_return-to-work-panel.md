Hosted by the Boston Women in Bioinformatics Advocacy Committee, this panel brings together women in bioinformatics who have navigated career breaks. They will share their experiences returning to work after voluntary, involuntary, or maternity leave.

## What You'll Get

Panelists will share how they approached their return to work, the challenges they faced along the way, and the advice they'd give to others navigating a similar journey.

## Who Should Attend

Anyone in the bioinformatics community interested in hearing stories about career transitions after time away, especially those considering or currently going through a career break themselves.
