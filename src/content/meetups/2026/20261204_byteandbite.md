## Details

​**For Women's HealthX Attendees Only**

Data, from how it’s collected to who can access it and how it’s analyzed, is one of the pillars of the Women’s HealthX agenda. This networking lunch aims to bring together data scientists, founders, clinicians, and anyone working with health data to collaborate on and discuss solutions to challenges the field faces: gaps in sex-specific and representative datasets, barriers to data access and sharing, and the analytical work needed to turn data into better care for women.

Come for the conversation, stay for the food, and leave with new connections across academia, industry, and the clinic.
