Come celebrate Cambridge's diverse, creative STEAM community together at the annual Cambridge Science Carnival. An "adventure playground" for inquisitive visitors of all ages, this family-friendly science extravaganza features more than 100 activity booths, demonstrations, and performances in just four hours.

## The Boston Women in Bioinformatics Booth: DNA by Design

Become a polymerase and make your own colorful DNA bracelet at our booth! Match the nucleotide beads, twist the strands into a double helix, and watch out because if our DNA inspectors spot a bead out of place you will need find and fix the mutation!

Younger scientists can create a simpler, single-stranded RNA-inspired bracelet by choosing colors and making their own pattern. Take home your wearable piece of science!

**Interested in volunteering at our booth? Reach out to Samantha Klasfeld or Iqra Amin on Slack!**
