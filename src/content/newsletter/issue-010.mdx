Registration is officially open for our 2026 Fall Fundraiser, **The Data, Tech, and Opportunities Driving a New Era of Women's Health**! Join us Thursday, October 1st at Eli Lilly in Seaport for an engaging panel, dinner, and networking with leaders across biotech, healthcare, and innovation, including [Ashley Abel, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ashleyabel), [Brandi Davis-Dusenbery PhD.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davis-dusenbery), [Shruthi Mahalingaiah, M.D., M.S.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shruthimahalingaiahmd) and [Ida Hatoum Moeller, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/idahatoum), and [Sheri Simmons, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sheri-simmons).

Every dataset, pipeline, and model reflects the assumptions of its creators. **What scientific knowledge might we have today if women's perspectives had been fully represented throughout history?** Our panelists will explore this question through the lens of federal policy, data infrastructure, reproductive and consumer health research, the $2.6B women's health VC landscape, and the future of the field.

**Early Bird pricing ($70 professional / $45 student) ends September 18th** — after that, prices go up to $80/$55, so grab your ticket now. This is one of our rare ticketed events, and proceeds directly support BWIB's year-round programming, networking events, and community resources.

**Student perk:** the first 25 student registrants get a FREE ticket to the [Women's HealthX Conference](https://boston-wib.org/events/womens-healthx-december-2026?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=fall-fundraiser-2026) this December ($99 value), including Career Zone access.

Boston Women in Bioinformatics extends our sincere thanks to the sponsors of our Fall Fundraiser: Gold sponsors [Seqera](https://seqera.io/), [Diamond Age](https://diamondage.com/), and [Zifo Technologies](https://zifo.com/); Silver sponsors [Benchling](https://www.benchling.com/) and [ZS](https://www.zs.com/); and Bronze sponsors [Sprout Informatics](https://www.sproutinformatics.com/), [Brandeis University](https://www.brandeis.edu/online), and [Bio Team](https://www.bioteam.net/). Their support makes our programming, events, and community possible, and we're grateful for their investment into our organization.

## Call for Volunteers: Cambridge Science Carnival

<div class="flex flex-col lg:flex-row lg:items-start gap-6">
  <img
    src="/photos/2025/science_carnival_2025_1.jpg"
    alt="Boston Women in Bioinformatics volunteers at a past Cambridge Science Carnival booth"
    width="250"
    class="mx-auto lg:mx-0 flex-shrink-0 rounded-lg"
  />

<div>

We're heading back to the [Cambridge Science Carnival](https://boston-wib.org/events/CambridgeScienceCarnival2026?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=cambridge-science-carnival-2026) on **Sunday, October 4th** with our "DNA by Design" booth — and we need volunteers to help run it!

</div>
</div>

Kids and adults alike will become "polymerases," matching colorful beads to build their own DNA (or a simpler single-stranded RNA) bracelet, all while our "DNA inspectors" keep an eye out for mutations to fix. It's one of our favorite ways to bring bioinformatics to families across Cambridge, and it takes a team to pull off.

**Interested in volunteering at our booth? Reach out to Samantha Klasfeld or Iqra Amin on Slack!**

## Coffee with CompBio: Academia or Industry?

<a
  href="https://boston-wib.org/blog/coffeewithcompbio/s2-e8?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=a-coffee-with-compbio"

Deciding between academia and industry is one of the biggest career forks a computational biology PhD faces. In our latest episode, host [Dr. Saba Nafees](https://www.linkedin.com/in/saba-nafees/) joins four other active members of our community: two scholars in academia ([Dr. Yaseswini Neelamraju, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yaseswinineelamraju) & [Dr. Dina Issakova, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dinaissakova)) and two scientists in industry ([Dr. Lina L. Faller, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/linafaller) & [Dr. Liyang Diao, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/liyang-diao-90933461/)) for an honest conversation about what life actually looks like in each space.

## Partner Events

**_BAGIM Happy Hour & Networking Event_**  
[Boston Area Group for Informatics and Modeling (BAGIM)](https://www.meetup.com/boston-area-group-for-informatics-and-modeling/) | Democracy Brewing, Boston, MA | Sep 16, 5:30 PM  
Join BAGIM at Democracy Brewing for a partner networking happy hour hosted alongside Revvity Signals and 20/15 Visioneers. Connect with fellow computational chemists, modelers, and informaticians across the biotech and pharma industries while enjoying complimentary food and drinks. More information [here](https://boston-wib.org/events/2026/20260916_bagim-networking-event?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=bagim-happy-hour).

**_Career Possibilities Panel & Speed Networking_**  
[WEST – Women in Enterprise of Science & Technology](https://www.westorg.org/) | Online via Zoom | Sep 30, 6:00 PM  
Join WEST for a virtual Career Possibilities Panel & Speed Networking event focused on AI, data science, and computational science. Hear panelists share their career paths and transferable skills, then join small-group speed networking sessions. More information [here](https://boston-wib.org/events/west-networking-zoom-sept-2026?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=west-networking-sept-2026).

**_Women's HealthX Conference_**  
Women's HealthX (WoHX) | Encore Boston Harbor, Boston, MA | Dec 3–4  
Women’s HealthX is a two-day conference for the leaders building the women’s health ecosystem from the ground up with sex-specific data, longitudinal research, and measurable outcomes that influence policy, reimbursement, product development, and clinical practice. You can find more information about tracks and registration here. **The first 25 student registrants for our [Fall Fundraiser](https://boston-wib.org/events/fall-fundraiser-2026?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=fall-fundraiser-2026) get a FREE ticket to this conference ($99 value), including Career Zone access.**
