Registration is officially open for our 2026 Fall Fundraiser, **The Data, Tech, and Opportunities Driving a New Era of Women's Health**! Join us Thursday, October 1st at Eli Lilly in Seaport for an engaging panel, dinner, and networking with leaders across biotech, healthcare, and innovation, including [Ashley Abel, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ashleyabel), [Brandi Davis-Dusenbery PhD.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davis-dusenbery), [Shruthi Mahalingaiah, M.D., M.S.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shruthimahalingaiahmd), [Ida Hatoum Moeller, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/idahatoum), and [Sheri Simmons, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sheri-simmons).

Every dataset, pipeline, and model reflects the assumptions of its creators. **What scientific knowledge might we have today if women's perspectives had been fully represented throughout history?** Our panelists will explore this question through the lens of federal policy, data infrastructure, reproductive and consumer health research, the $2.6B women's health VC landscape, and the future of the field.

**Early Bird pricing ($70 professional / $45 student) ends September 18th** — after that, prices go up to $80/$55, so grab your ticket now. This is one of our rare ticketed events, and proceeds directly support BWIB's year-round programming, networking events, and community resources.

## Call for Volunteers: Cambridge Science Carnival

We're heading back to the [Cambridge Science Carnival](https://boston-wib.org/events/CambridgeScienceCarnival2026?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=cambridge-science-carnival-2026) on **Sunday, October 4th** with our "DNA by Design" booth — and we need volunteers to help run it!

## Don't Lose Us in Spam

To ensure you never miss an update, add **newsletter@boston-wib.org** and **communications@boston-wib.org** to your email contacts.

For guaranteed delivery, click below to see specific steps for your provider:

<details class="image-description">
<summary>Instructions</summary>

### Gmail (Desktop)

1. Click **Settings (Gear icon)** > **See all settings**.
2. Go to **Filters and Blocked Addresses** > **Create a new filter**.
3. In the **From** field, paste: `newsletter@boston-wib.org OR communications@boston-wib.org`
4. Click **Create filter**, check **Never send it to Spam**, and click **Create filter** again.

### Outlook (Web)

1. Click **Settings (Gear icon)** > **Mail** > **Junk email**.
2. Under **Safe senders and domains**, click **Add**.
3. Type `boston-wib.org` and press **Enter**.
4. Click **Save**.

### Apple Mail (iPhone/Mac)

1. Open the **Contacts** app and click **+** to create a new contact.
2. Name it **Boston WIB**.
3. Add both email addresses to the contact card and save.

</details>

_Tip: If an email is already in your Spam folder, open it and select **Not Spam** or drag it to your Primary inbox to train your provider._
