## Introducing the BWIB Women+ Leader Spotlight Series

_[Dedeepya Mullapudi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dedeepyaurl/), Boston Women in Bioinformatics Mentorship Committee_

The Boston Women in Bioinformatics (BWIB) Mentorship Committee is excited to introduce our **Women+ Leader Spotlight Series**, a new initiative highlighting the career journeys, experiences, and insights of accomplished women+ leaders in bioinformatics and related fields. Through these interviews, we hope to inspire students and early-career professionals by sharing diverse career paths, leadership lessons, and practical advice.

## Events Reflections

### Byte & Bite

_Beatriz Bergamo_

<figure>
  <img
    src="/photos/2026/byteNbite_nov2026.jpg"
    alt="Byte & Bite attendees smiling around a long wooden table at Time Out Market Boston"

This was my first Byte & Bite and my first event with Boston Women in Bioinformatics, and I'm so glad I went! It was really nice to meet people from both academia and industry, at different stages of their careers, and hear about what they're working on.

We also got to hear from Melisa Osborne about her experience as a lecturer and researcher at Boston University. During our conversation, we talked about how challenging it can be to switch between different projects and datasets, especially when each has its own limitations and requires a different approach to analysis. It was interesting to hear how different people deal with this. Melisa suggested blocking off time for specific projects when possible, but also pointed out that it takes some trial and error to figure out what works for you.

I'm really glad I didn't let the rain stop me from going! Thanks to [Liyang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/liyang-diao-90933461/) for hosting!

### Return to Work Panel

_[Viveka Patil](https://www.linkedin.com/in/viveka-patil-934b3123b), Boston Women in Bioinformatics Enabling Advocacy Committee Co-chair_

<figure>
  <img
    src="/photos/2026/return-to-work-panel-2026.png"
    alt="Panelists and attendees of the Return to Work Panel on a video call"
    width="512"
    height="252"
    class="mx-auto rounded-lg"
  />
  <figcaption>Panelists and attendees at the Return to Work Panel on October 17th.</figcaption>
</figure>

The job market has been tough for everyone, and especially for those trying to get their foot back in the door after a break. Our Return to Work panel set out to connect people who have been through a career break and returned to work with peers who are looking to re-enter the job market now. Over one hour, panelists Laurel Wright, Ph.D., Katie Hughes, and [Prisni Rath](https://www.linkedin.com/in/prisni-r/) shared their personal journeys back to work, along with practical advice on understanding severance, building a financial backup fund, and handling immigration-related concerns. The conversation also turned to postpartum stress and anxiety, and the value of finding a group of peers to lean on.

The panel closed with an audience Q&A that highlighted red flags to watch for during the interview process and shared helpful resources: [Hiring Cafe](https://hiringcafe.com/) (an AI-powered job search site recommended over Indeed), [Never Search Alone](https://www.neversearchalone.org/) (a free book and peer group community), LinkedIn for cold outreach, online MBA programs for those pivoting from scientific to commercial roles, and postpartum or neighborhood mom support groups, which pediatricians can often refer you to.

## Don't Lose Us in Spam

To ensure you never miss an update, add **newsletter@boston-wib.org** and **communications@boston-wib.org** to your email contacts.

For guaranteed delivery, click below to see specific steps for your provider:

<details class="image-description">
<summary>Instructions</summary>

### Gmail (Desktop)

1. Click **Settings (Gear icon)** > **See all settings**.
2. Go to **Filters and Blocked Addresses** > **Create a new filter**.
3. In the **From** field, paste: `newsletter@boston-wib.org OR communications@boston-wib.org`
4. Click **Create filter**, check **Never send it to Spam**, and click **Create filter** again.

### Outlook (Web)

1. Click **Settings (Gear icon)** > **Mail** > **Junk email**.
2. Under **Safe senders and domains**, click **Add**.
3. Type `boston-wib.org` and press **Enter**.
4. Click **Save**.

### Apple Mail (iPhone/Mac)

1. Open the **Contacts** app and click **+** to create a new contact.
2. Name it **Boston WIB**.
3. Add both email addresses to the contact card and save.

</details>

_Tip: If an email is already in your Spam folder, open it and select **Not Spam** or drag it to your Primary inbox to train your provider._
